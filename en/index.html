01Learn more

töre: an open-source, local notebook. It helps people keep their thoughts as their own when they use artificial intelligence (AI).

Open source: anyone can read the source code. Local: the desktop version of töre keeps your data on your own computer.
How to say it: “tö-reh” [tœˈɾe]. The ö is like the vowel in “bird”. The stress is on the second syllable. töre is a Turkish word. It means how a community lives together.

02The problem

The owner of a thought becomes unclearunclear

You may think together with AI. After a short time, it can become unclear which sentence is yours and which is the AI's.

Later, you may return to a decision. Then it is harder to find who suggested it and when you accepted it. As the number of conversations grows, you can also lose the source of a thought.

03How it works

Three voices stay separate

Works today the desktop version of töre (preview)

The desktop version of töre keeps the decisions, views and open questions from your conversations with AI. It keeps them together with their sources. In each record, the three voices stay separate. Nothing becomes your view until you approve it.

Source
The conversation itself: when it happened, in which tool, and who said what.
Your words
Only the sentences that you choose and approve go into your thought map.
AI suggestion
It stays marked as a suggestion. It does not become your view until you accept it.

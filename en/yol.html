02Future map

The future: two paths, goals without dates

Product path

  1. 1Goal

    A place for content

    Some people love to learn about their favorite topics and want to share what they learn. These people create content on töre. Other people do not want to share. They follow the people who create content on their favorite topics.

  2. 2Goal

    Assistant

    töre becomes an assistant. It connects to every post that a person makes on social media. It becomes the place where all of a person's posts and views come together.

Independence path

  1. 1GoalLooking for investment

    Economic independence for Önder Samet

    To be able to focus fully on töre. The plan for this is to get investment. The search for investment has started.

  2. 2Goal

    Economic freedom for content creators

    töre reaches a level where its content creators can also gain economic freedom.

Final goal

A large language model starts to use töre.

05Words and their dates

When the words first appeared

The word töre appears in the Orkhon inscriptions. There, it means order, law and custom. The words of töre first appeared in conversations, before the first version. The dates come from those conversations, from notes and from the code repository.

  1. 11 November 2026

    The first idea

    An idea for social media where people share reports written with AI.

  2. 13 November 2026

    Törü

    The project name appeared for the first time, in this spelling. The first version was Törü 0.1.1: a local Markdown archive.

  3. 14 November 2026

    töre

    The name became final as “töre”.

  4. 15 November 2026

    First code record

    The first record in the code repository: töre 0.8.0.

  5. 15 November 2026

    Three voices

    The name for a principle: the source, your words and the AI suggestion stay separate.

  6. 26 November 2026

    Closed trial

    The trial was decided: it would start on 15 December with a small number of people.

  7. 26 November 2026

    Sayım

    The name of the section where income and spending will be counted in public.

07Proof

Check it yourself

The desktop version of töre keeps your decisions together with their sources. In the same way, töre keeps a record of its own work. You can check the two items below in the documents on GitHub. Unfinished work shows its status next to it. The documents on GitHub are in English.

Local data

The desktop version of töre keeps its records and your thought map in a folder on your computer. Today, it is a preview, that is, an early version. The source code is open, so you can check this yourself.

Scope: This is true for the desktop version. For the trial: in development.

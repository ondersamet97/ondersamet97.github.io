Who develops töre

töre is a project that Önder Samet develops.

töre is independent. It is not yet a company with legal status. Its source code is open to everyone. Artificial intelligence (AI) coding tools help to develop töre.

Send an email: ondersamet1997@gmail.com

Write to töre on LinkedIn

You can reach Önder Samet only by email or on the LinkedIn page of töre. This site does not ask you for any information.

Who is Önder Samet

Önder Samet develops töre. He works with AI every day. But AI does not make his decisions. AI prepares the discussion. A person makes the decision. töre came from this principle.

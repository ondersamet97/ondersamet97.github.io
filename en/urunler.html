Desktop version
Preview, open source (AGPL). To use it, you install it on your computer from the source code. The latest published source code is version 0.18.0 (18 November 2026). It is on GitHub.

The part of töre that works today is the desktop version. It is an open-source, local notebook (preview). Local means that your data stays on your own computer. A preview is an early version.

In the closed trial, töre is becoming a place where participants see each other and discuss topics.

Try it

Do you want to join the trial today? The contact address is on the Roadmap page.

The desktop version keeps the decisions, views and open questions from your conversations with artificial intelligence (AI). It keeps them together with their sources. The source conversation, your words and the AI suggestion stay separate. Nothing becomes your view without your approval.

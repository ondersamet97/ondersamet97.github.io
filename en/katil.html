Why should you join töre?

töre is becoming a place to discuss the topics that interest you. You can discuss them there with people who have the same interests.

Four reasons to join

  1. 1

    töre is becoming a place to discuss your topic with people who care about it

    In the closed trial, participants will see each other and discuss topics.

    Closed trial: 15 December (a small number of people, by invitation) · the app is in development

    • LinkThe trial, on the roadmap
  2. 2

    Your thoughts stay yours

    When you think with AI (artificial intelligence) in töre, the final decision is yours. A sentence becomes your view when you choose and approve it. töre marks each AI suggestion with the word “suggestion”. Your thought map stays on your own computer.

    Works today in the desktop version of töre (preview)

    Your wordsYou approved

    AI suggestionWaits as a suggestion

    Scope: This is true for the desktop version of töre. For the trial: in development.

    • ProofThe principles “Nothing becomes your view silently” and “Local by default” (GitHub)
    • LinkHow the three voices stay separate
  3. 3

    töre does its work in public

    The source code is open (AGPL). The version history and the roadmap are public. töre marks unfinished work as “in development”. töre is developing Sayım for its money. The name means “count” in Turkish. Under Sayım, töre counts in public all the money that comes in and goes out.

  4. 4

    töre is worth your attention

    töre does not compete for your attention. It has no endless notifications, no badges and no designs that mislead you. This site also uses no cookies and no tracking. This site loads nothing from other sites.

How to join

In development

The way to join is in development. The closed trial is by invitation. Do you want to join the trial today? The contact address is on the Roadmap page.

Try it

The app is in development. There is no announcement yet about what the trial will share. There is also no announcement yet about where the trial data will stay.

Today: what you can do

You can do two things today.

1Tell people about töre

Copy this text. Send it to people who may have an interest in töre.

Text to share

töre is a place where you can discuss the topics that interest you with people who have the same interests.

#töre

When you talk about töre, use the tag #töre. Add the tag at the end of the text that you share. This way, töre can find the people who talk about it.

2Help töre find support

Tell people and organizations about töre if they can support töre. People who want to contact töre can write on the LinkedIn page.

Right now, the best way to help is to follow töre.

Follow töre on LinkedIn

Other ways

Feedback In development

The way to give feedback is in development. Today, you can write to töre about an error, a suggestion or an objection. Use email or the LinkedIn page of töre. Do not send your real conversations or your personal notes.

Contact: Who develops töre

Contributions In development

For now, töre does not accept code from other people. The agreement for people who give code to töre is not ready yet. Do you want to give code? First, send an email or a message on LinkedIn and explain your plan.

Contribution guide (GitHub)

Sayım In development

töre counts in public all the money that comes in and goes out. There is no income yet. Sayım does not promise payment or a part of the income to anyone.
